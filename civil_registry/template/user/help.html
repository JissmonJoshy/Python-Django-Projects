Civil Registry - Application Guidelines


Birth Certificate Application:

  1. Visit the Civil Registry website and log in to your account.
  2. Locate the "Birth Certificate" from "apply" section.or click here
  3. Fill out the birth certificate application form with accurate information.
  4. Provide details such as the name of the child, date and place of birth, and parent's information.
  5. Attach the required documents, including proof of birth and identification documents of the child and parents.
  6. Submit the application form and documents.
  7. Make the payment for the service charges, if applicable.
  8. Track the application status at"Birth Certificate" from "Track Status" section.or click here
  9. Once approved,to download the digital copy of the birth certificate click here
