Civil Registry - Application Guidelines

Registration and Login:

  1. Visit the Civil Registry website and click on the "Register" button to create a new account.
  2. Provide the required information, including your email and password, to complete the registration process.
  3. Once registered, click on the "Login" button and enter your credentials to access your account.

Application Form Submission:

  1. After logging in, navigate to the appropriate section based on the type of certificate you are applying for (e.g., "Birth Certificate Application").
  2. Fill out the application form with accurate and complete information.
  3. Ensure all mandatory fields are filled in correctly, including personal details, parent information, and relevant dates.
  4. Review the form to confirm the accuracy of the provided information.

Document Upload:

  1. Prepare the necessary documents required for the certificate application (e.g., proof of birth, identification documents, parents' details).
  2. Scan or take clear photos of the documents for digital submission.
  3. On the application form, locate the designated document upload section.
  4. Click on the "Choose File" or similar button to select the corresponding document files from your device.
  5. Upload the documents as instructed, ensuring that they meet the specified file format and size requirements.

Service Charge Payment:

  1. Upon completing the application form and document upload, you will be directed to the payment section.
  2. The system will calculate the service charge based on the selected certificate type and any additional fees.
  3. Choose the preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, online banking).
  4. Enter the required payment details accurately and securely.
  5. Follow the prompts to complete the payment process.
  6. Upon successful payment, you will receive a payment confirmation.

Application Tracking:

  1. After submitting the application and making the payment, you can track the status of your application.
  2. Log in to your account and navigate to the "Application Status" or similar section.
  3. Enter the unique application ID or other relevant details to access your application status.
  4. The system will display the current status of your application, such as "Pending Review," "In Progress," "Approved," or "Rejected."
  5. Regularly check the status for any updates or notifications regarding your application.

Document Download and Postal Delivery:

  1. Once your application is approved, you can download the digital version of your certificate.
  2. Visit the application status page and locate the "Download Certificate" button.
  3. Click on the button to download the PDF copy of your birth, death, marriage, or other certificate.
  4. If requested, provide the postal address during the application process.
  5. Your physical copy of the certificate will be delivered to the provided address.
